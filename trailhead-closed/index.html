Trail Access & Parking

Trail access remains open via a designated route. Certain trail sections along active work are closed and posted. Please follow all signs and barricades.

Please use alternate parking at:

  • 27th Street Trailhead
  • 29th Street Trailhead

What to Expect

  • Construction begins at the west end of the project area and moves toward 22nd St. See the map below for details.
  • Ongoing excavation and construction along the trail, with noticeable noise and dust.
  • Daytime work hours: Monday – Friday, Saturday as needed.
  • Follow all posted signs and barricades for your safety.

Important notice — heavy truck traffic

Expect a significant increase in large construction trucks on 21st and 22nd St.

  • Trucks will be traveling in and out frequently throughout the day.
  • Please use extra caution when driving, walking, or biking.
  • Expect delays, noise, and dust.
