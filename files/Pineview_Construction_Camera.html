📢 Media: permission required before using the video
This is an active construction site. Timelapse video may not be broadcast, streamed or posted until the project team has responded to your request. Clicking Download MP4 downloads the video and opens a pre-addressed email to info@ogdencitypipeline.com. Enter your name, phone and media outlet name and send it. The construction contractor is copied on every request. Video use permission is granted only after you receive a response from the project team. If no email window opens, send the same details to that address with the subject “Request to utilize Pineview Crossing Video.”
Still photos: may be used in broadcast or online media without an email request, provided they are credited — "Courtesy of Ogden City Engineering." The full-screen view is intended for control rooms and on-air display. The timelapse is a rolling 12-second loop of the last 10 hours, rebuilt about every 30 minutes — it is not live video.
