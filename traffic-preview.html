Project Traffic Control

What our crews are doing on the road right now.

Short-distance traffic control in the upper canyon

Flaggers may control one lane of SR-39 near the Pineview Water Treatment Plant and the SR-39 crossing as crews install the new 36-inch pipeline toward The Oaks Restaurant. Short, intermittent traffic control may also be in place on SR-158 on the west side of Pineview Reservoir. Please slow down, follow the flaggers, and allow a few extra minutes.

For UDOT review: what this page uses (remove before launch)
  • Cameras (5 + 2): images loaded directly from udottraffic.utah.gov, refreshed every 60 seconds; each links back to UDOT. No images are copied or stored by us.
